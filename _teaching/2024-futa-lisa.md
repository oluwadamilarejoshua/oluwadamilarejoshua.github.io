At FUTA-LISA, the Department of Statistics' analytical laboratory, I lead statistical analysis projects brought by researchers across the university and teach data analytics. Across 2020 – 2021 and 2024 – present I have trained more than 150 learners from beginner to intermediate level, designing the R curriculum and co-organising hackathons. I also teach in the laboratory's three-month data analytics programme for recent graduates, covering R, Python, and SQL.
