As a Research and Teaching Assistant (May 2024 – December 2025), I delivered lectures for STA224, covering descriptive and inferential statistics, measures of central tendency and dispersion, hypothesis testing, Bayes' theorem, and introductory linear regression. I also graded continuous assessments, including assignments, class work, and participation, and trained penultimate-year student interns in Excel, SPSS, Stata, and R.
