I am a statistician working on **time series analysis**, **Bayesian inference**, and **financial econometrics**. My research asks how estimation choices shape what we learn from volatility models, and how macroeconomic and fiscal fundamentals drive financial risk.

I am completing an **MTech in Statistics** at the [Federal University of Technology, Akure (FUTA)](https://futa.edu.ng/) (CGPA 4.53/5.0, Distinction expected October 2026), advised by [Prof. Oluwadare O. Ojo](https://sta.futa.edu.ng/home/profile/1831) and mentored by [Prof. O. A. Adeoti](https://sta.futa.edu.ng/home/profile/3152). I am also a **Senior Coordinator, Data Scientist** at [eHealth Africa](https://www.ehealthafrica.org/), where I lead a research portfolio of five spatial-epidemiology studies.

**I am applying to PhD programmes for 2027 entry.** My [CV](/cv/) and [research](/publications/) are below.

Research
======

- **Likelihood approximation for regime-switching volatility models.** My MTech thesis compares four likelihood approximations for Markov-switching GARCH models across thirty assets in five markets, using a C++ likelihood engine I built for the purpose. It shows that the choice of approximation, which applied work rarely reports, changes the results. *Accepted for poster presentation at CFE-CMStatistics 2026, Berlin.*
- **Bayesian volatility modelling of exchange rates.** A Bayesian EGARCH-X model, estimated with Hamiltonian Monte Carlo, shows that debt servicing and GDP growth drive the *risk* of the Nigerian naira rather than its level.
- **Forecasting and causality in energy markets.** Published work on crude-oil price forecasting (*Studies of Applied Economics*, 2026) and on energy-growth causality in five oil-exporting African economies (*JRSS Nigeria Group*, 2024).
