A survey of final-year undergraduates at the Federal University of Technology, Akure, using the Hospital Anxiety and Depression Scale to estimate the prevalence of anxiety and depression and to identify associated sociodemographic and academic factors.

**My role.** I conceived the study, designed the questionnaire, analysed the data in R, and wrote the draft.

Co-authors: Adebimpe Aladeniyi (FUTA), Kathryn Baughman (DeliBerate Strategies LLC), Olumide Arigbede (US Food and Drug Administration), and Tope Amusa (Georgia State University). [Session page](https://apha.confex.com/apha/2025/meetingapp.cgi/Session/74493).
