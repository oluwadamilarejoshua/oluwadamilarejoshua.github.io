**Summary.** We forecast daily WTI and Brent crude-oil prices with three techniques, each suited to a different part of the problem: GARCH for volatility clustering, Support Vector Regression for short-term price prediction, and an LSTM network for long-term trends. GARCH captures volatility patterns best, making it the natural tool for risk and market-shock analysis. SVR performs best for short-term prediction under stable market conditions, and LSTM is most valuable for long-horizon forecasts, particularly in volatile periods. No single model dominates, so the choice of method should follow the forecasting horizon and market conditions.

**My role.** I implemented the GARCH, SVR, and LSTM models in R and contributed to the comparative analysis.
